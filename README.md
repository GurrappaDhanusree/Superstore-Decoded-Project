### 📌 Project Overview
This is an end-to-end data analysis project using the classic Superstore dataset. The goal was to analyze why high sales are not converting into high profits and to identify loss-making areas.

### 🎯 Business Problem
- Some categories have high sales but low/negative profit
- High discounts are eating into profit margin
- Need to identify which States and Sub-Categories are causing loss

### 📊 Dashboard Contains
- **Trend Analysis:** Year-over-Year Sales & Profit Growth
- **Category Analysis:** Category & Sub-Category wise Performance
- **Geographical Analysis:** State-wise Profit Map
- **Risk Analysis:** Discount vs Profit Relationship

### 💡 Key Insights Found
- **Technology** is the most profitable category, while **Tables** are in loss
- Discount > 25% leads to negative profit
- **California** has highest sales, but **Texas** has highest loss
- **Consumer Segment** contributes 52% of total sales
- West Region is most profitable
