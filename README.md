# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

An end-to-end Tableau project analyzing Superstore performance to improve profitability.

### Business Problem
High sales but low profit in some categories due to high discounts.

### Dashboard Includes
- KPIs: Sales, Profit, Profit Margin
- Sales & Profit Trend (YoY)
- Category & Sub-Category Performance
- State-wise Profit Map
- Discount vs Profit Analysis

### Key Insights
- Technology is the most profitable category
- Tables are in loss due to >25% discount
- California leads in sales, Texas leads in loss
- Consumer segment = 52% of total sales

### Tools Used
Tableau Desktop, Excel
